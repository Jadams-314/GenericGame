Going right...

You find yourself in the forest of text-based games.

Trees surround you from both sides on a seemingly endless treck into a seemingly endless path.

But this isn't computer science class with decision trees that you can trace the root node of.

This is a forest of pure, 100% text. The scariest thing known to humanity since the second derivative.

Your head begins to ache as copy-pastas seep into your skull.
